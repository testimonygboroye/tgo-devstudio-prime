# Nigerian technology-services market benchmark research

**Research checked:** 27 November 2026  
**Currency:** NGN  
**Purpose:** Evidence for founder review and the TGO commercial catalogue. These are publicly advertised provider prices and published planning ranges, not an independently audited national price index and not TGO selling prices.

## Method

- Prefer Nigerian providers publishing a price, package or dated pricing guide.
- Record whether a figure is a fixed starting price, an advertised package, a planning range, or a monthly retainer.
- Do not treat a provider's own price as the national average.
- Where a source gives a range, the benchmark low/high retain that range. A midpoint may be stored as a comparison reference only and must be labelled as a calculated midpoint, not an observed market average.
- Match benchmarks to the scope actually described. Do not transfer a landing-page price to a complex web application, or a mobile-app price to desktop software.
- If no sufficiently comparable public Nigerian price is available, leave the benchmark unverified and use custom quotation rather than fabricating a figure.

## Families that must remain custom-quote / benchmark-unverified

The sources reviewed did not provide sufficiently comparable, independently checkable Nigerian public price ranges for these specific scopes. Do not populate numerical market averages until comparable provider quotes or dated public rate cards are collected:

- Desktop Software Development
- SaaS & Digital Products (standalone subscription products vary by seat, usage and support; do not confuse SaaS subscription price with product engineering cost)
- AI Agents (distinct from a basic chatbot or knowledge-base assistant)
- Cloud Infrastructure implementation (separate from cloud hosting bills and a DevOps audit)
- Quality Assurance & Software Testing
- IoT & Connected Systems
- Advanced Digital Technologies (blockchain, AR/VR and similar specialist work)

These are deliberately marked unverified rather than assigned invented prices. A service can still be sold through a scoped TGO quotation.

## Catalogue entry policy

- Keep market benchmark evidence separate from TGO's own package prices.
- Store the checked date, currency, methodology and source URLs on every benchmark.
- Preserve the source's pricing basis (one-off, monthly, annual percentage, component-level or starting price) in the methodology/notes.
- Never use a broad family benchmark as a fixed checkout price.
- Where the service scope does not match the evidence, leave the benchmark empty and route the customer to a scoped quotation.
