# Nigerian software-services market evidence register

**Research checked:** 27 November 2026  
**Currency:** NGN  
**Purpose:** Evidence register for TGO's commercial catalogue. These are observed public provider prices and indicative comparison bands, not a claim that every vendor delivers identical scope. They must not be presented as a universal market average or automatically copied into TGO selling prices.

## Method

- Prefer publicly visible Nigerian provider price pages or dated Nigeria-specific pricing guides.
- Record the provider, exact public offer/range, scope caveat, source URL and checked date.
- Compare only reasonably similar scopes. Where scopes differ materially, retain the observations as context rather than calculating a misleading average.
- Use a directional low/high and median only where at least three comparable public observations exist; otherwise leave the benchmark unfilled pending more evidence.
- Re-check prices before publishing or quoting because providers can change packages and NGN prices.

## Categories not yet benchmarked

No market benchmark is asserted in this register for the remaining service families, including consulting, web applications, desktop software, SaaS, UI/UX, backend/API, data engineering, AI, AI agents, automation, cloud, DevOps/SRE, cybersecurity, QA, enterprise systems, integrations, maintenance, modernisation, technical SEO, IoT, advanced technologies, and technical training.

Those categories require category-specific comparable sources and scope normalization. Leave their benchmark values empty until that work is completed. Do not extrapolate website or mobile prices into unrelated services.

## Catalogue publication rule

A benchmark may be saved only with:
1. A low, high and typical value in the stated currency, satisfying low ≤ typical ≤ high.
2. A checked date.
3. A written methodology describing sample, scope and how the figures were derived.
4. At least two labelled HTTPS sources, with a note explaining the compared offer.

Keep market benchmarks separate from TGO's own package prices. TGO package pricing remains a commercial decision and must be justified by scope, delivery effort, quality, risk and support—not copied blindly from competitor prices.
